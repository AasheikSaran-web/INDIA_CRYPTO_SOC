Setup Timing — Fully Met

Both frequency targets achieved zero setup violations. 500 MHz: +0.001 ns WNS (1 ps margin). 1 GHz: 0.000 ns WNS (razor-tight). Critical path is the 64-bit MDU accumulate chain.

⚠️
Pre-CTS Hold Violations

1325 hold violations at 500 MHz, 1233 at 1 GHz — all pre-CTS, concentrated in u_trng sync regs and u_cpu ifid_instr. Will be fixed by hold buffers during Place & Route.

Area Efficiency

1 GHz incurs only +109 μm² (+0.18%) area penalty over 500 MHz. Total cell area: 60,519 μm². u_aes_ca dominates at 49% area, u_cpu leads power at 18.6%.
